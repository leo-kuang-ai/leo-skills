# 图片渲染：幻想动画（fantasy-animation）

**分类:** canonical/axes/rendering · 特色

**配对视觉风格:** paper-cut / sketch-notes

**定位:** Ghibli/Disney-inspired hand-drawn animation warmth. Soft painterly forms, gentle lighting, expressive but simplified characters, dreamy atmosphere. Used for children's content, storybook brand narratives, healing/comforting themes, fairy-tale or whimsical decks.

## 1. 风格段落（paste-ready，可直接用于图片生成）

> Hand-drawn animation style inspired by Ghibli and classic Disney storybook aesthetics. Forms are softly rendered with painterly fills and gentle outlines — never harsh. Core colors are anchored by the deck roles; dreamy warmth may derive through soft window light, gentle glow, morning haze, brush texture, atmospheric perspective, and coherent pastel/earth-tone transitions. A central character is supported by a small companion creature and floating thematic objects (books, bags, boxes, orbs), all stylized in a friendly cartoon manner — large expressive eyes, simplified anatomy, warm body language. Foreground forms are richer and the background softer. Overall feel is dreamy, magical, comforting, and storybook-like. Rendering guardrails: no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks inside the image — any typography is applied later by the page layout, never by the image model. Compose for a 16:9 widescreen canvas (2560×1440): wide horizontal frame, not a poster column.

## 2. 线条 · 纹理 · 深度

| 维度 | 处理 |
|---|---|
| 线条质量 | Soft hand-drawn outlines or no outlines, with painterly fills inside |
| 纹理 | Subtle painterly brush quality + atmospheric softness |
| 深度 | Strong atmospheric perspective — distant elements paler and softer |
| 材质 | Hand-painted animation cel |
| 情绪 | Dreamy, magical, comforting, storybook |

> 色值来自 deck 的 `colors` 锚点，本渲染只描述画法，不写死 HEX。
