# 图片渲染：街拍纪实瞬间（candid-street-moment）

**分类:** canonical/axes/rendering · 叙事氛围

**配对视觉风格:** 公益组织风（纪实人本气质共享）;亦可无配对直接点名,默认使用场景见 `template-library/governance/authoring/index/视觉风格配对.md` 主表

**定位:** 未摆拍的手机视角街拍瞬间—— plausible 机位高度、轻微运动模糊、环境上下文与纪实张力,反对广告级摆拍抛光

## 1. 风格段落（paste-ready，可直接用于图片生成）

> A spontaneous unscripted street moment captured like a phone photo from a plausible camera height. Imperfect framing with contextual street detail, a slight motion blur on the moving subject, and available light only. The frame keeps small real-world imperfections and documentary tension — never staged advertising polish, never studio lighting. Rendering guardrails: no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks inside the image — any typography is applied later by the page layout, never by the image model. Compose for a 16:9 widescreen canvas (2560×1440): wide horizontal frame, not a poster column.

## 2. 线条 · 纹理 · 深度

| 维度 | 处理 |
|---|---|
| 线条质量 | Natural photographic edges; no graphic outlines |
| 纹理 | Real street grain, honest skin and fabric texture |
| 深度 | Ambient depth of field; subject separated by focus, not glow |
| 材质 | Available-light photography; phone-camera honesty |
| 情绪 | Candid, unscripted, documentary tension |

> 渲染画法不写死 HEX;deck `colors` 锚点缺省时按风格 brief 的主色系走自然灰阶与暖肤色。适用场景（源自卡 tags）：纪实页 / 人物故事页 / 场景氛围页 / 公益与城市叙事。
