# 图片渲染：杂志编辑（editorial）

**分类:** canonical/axes/rendering · 现代商业

**配对视觉风格:** 创意杂志风 / 电子墨水杂志风（图表样式见 `template-library/governance/authoring/index/图表样式规范.md`）

**定位:** Magazine-style infographic look — sophisticated layout, refined type-as-shape elements, restrained color, editorial pacing. The default choice for finance, journalism, explainers, opinion pieces, sophisticated B2B content.

## 1. 风格段落（paste-ready，可直接用于图片生成）

> Magazine-style editorial illustration with sophisticated visual hierarchy and confident negative space. Elements are arranged with editorial pacing — one dominant focal area, deliberate alignment to invisible columns, generous gutters between blocks. Color is restrained and balanced — typically one rich brand tone, one neutral, one subtle accent. Geometric forms are simple but rendered with editorial confidence: bold blocks, refined dividers, accent rules. Optional duotone or limited-color treatment reinforces the magazine feel. Composition feels like a thoughtful magazine spread — not loud, not minimal, but considered. Overall mood is intelligent, premium, restrained, suitable for analytical and opinion content. Rendering guardrails: no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks inside the image — any typography is applied later by the page layout, never by the image model. Compose for a 16:9 widescreen canvas (2560×1440): wide horizontal frame, not a poster column.

## 2. 线条 · 纹理 · 深度

| 维度 | 处理 |
|---|---|
| 线条质量 | Refined — thin precise rules as dividers; clean filled shapes |
| 纹理 | Minimal — optional very subtle paper grain at 5-10% opacity |
| 深度 | Flat with intentional layering; no shadows |
| 材质 | None — abstract editorial composition |
| 情绪 | Refined, intelligent, premium-restrained |

> 色值来自 deck 的 `colors` 锚点，本渲染只描述画法，不写死 HEX。
