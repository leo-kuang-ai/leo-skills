# 图片渲染：渐变手绘题头（handdrawn-gradient-header）

**分类:** canonical/axes/rendering · 手绘教育

**配对视觉风格:** —（本批新增渲染,无固定 01 配对;可直接点名,默认使用场景见 `template-library/governance/authoring/index/视觉风格配对.md` 末节）

**定位:** 蓝绿渐变冲洗的松弛手绘插画题头——斜体速写线 + 标题带并置,解释型内容的人味开场

## 1. 风格段落（paste-ready，可直接用于图片生成）

> Loose hand-drawn illustration washed in a blue-to-green gradient, with light italic sketch linework and an approachable, human feel. A simple illustrated character or motif sits beside a clear title band in 16:9, like the header of a well-designed explainer. Casual, warm, and inviting — good for intros, section openers, and lightweight topics. Rendering guardrails: no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks inside the image — any typography is applied later by the page layout, never by the image model. Compose for a 16:9 widescreen canvas (2560×1440): wide horizontal frame, not a poster column.

## 2. 线条 · 纹理 · 深度

| 维度 | 处理 |
|---|---|
| 线条质量 | Light italic sketch linework, loose and unpolished |
| 纹理 | Blue-to-green gradient wash over sketch strokes |
| 深度 | Flat wash with layering only between motif and band |
| 材质 | Marker-style ink under watercolor-grade gradient |
| 情绪 | Casual, warm, inviting, human |

> 渲染画法不写死 HEX;源卡参考色板 `#2FBF9F / #2E86AB / #F5F7F2（首色为 accent）` 与信息密度 `low` 仅作 deck `colors` 锚点缺省时的默认建议。适用场景（源自卡 tags）：章节开篇 / 解释型 intro / 轻量主题 / 教育培训。
